Roof replacement in Blowing Rock, NC

Our replacements are full tear-offs, never an overlay, using GAF shingles such as Timberline HDZ and Camelot II designer shingles, with synthetic underlayment and ridge vents. Roofing permits in Blowing Rock

Inside Blowing Rock town limits, the Town of Blowing Rock Planning & Inspections issues building permits (1038 Main St, (828) 295-5240). Blowing Rock straddles Watauga and Caldwell counties, and outside town limits the county handles permits; on the Watauga side that’s Watauga County Planning & Inspections (126 Poplar Grove Connector, Suite 201, Boone, (828) 265-8043). Many residential re-roofs in NC under $40,000 don’t need a building permit (G.S. 160D-1110(c)), but the Town of Blowing Rock or the county decides, and we check before work starts.
