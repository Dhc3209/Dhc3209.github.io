Lake Norman wind and hail — what we look for

Waterfront and near-lake roofs take different wind loads and weather cycles. We inspect for lifted shingles, bruised tabs, flashing failures, and soft decking — then document it clearly for you and your adjuster.

Cornelius waterfront and near-lake roofs see open-water wind that inland Mecklenburg lots often don’t.
