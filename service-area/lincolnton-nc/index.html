Roofing permits in Lincolnton

Lincoln County Planning & Inspections issues building permits for Lincolnton and the rest of the county (704-736-8440). If a home is inside Lincolnton city limits or its extraterritorial jurisdiction (ETJ), the City of Lincolnton Planning Department (704-736-8930) must give zoning approval before the county will issue a building permit.
