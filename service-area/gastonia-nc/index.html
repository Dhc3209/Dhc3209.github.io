Roof replacement in Gastonia

Replacement means the existing roof comes off. We tear off to the deck, replace sheathing that will not hold a fastener or that has gone soft, and build the roof back as a GAF system: underlayment, flashing at walls, chimneys, and penetrations, and the shingles in that scope. Qualifying Master Elite installs can include an enhanced GAF warranty. You hear the scope and the cleanup plan before the tear-off is scheduled.

The wider notes are on roof repair and replacement. Roofs that should be metal are on metal roofing, and metal means MRS.

Roof repair

A Gastonia repair is for damage that is actually local: lifted tabs on one eave, a boot cracked at a vent, counterflashing that has left the mortar, or a tired valley beside fields that are still sound. If the slopes around that spot are at the end of their life, a neat patch is the wrong product. We would rather replace the roof once than sell a repair we do not believe in.

Hail in Gaston County

The hail report on this page is not in the city of Gastonia. NOAA event 1279166 is half-dollar hail, 1.25 inch, at Cherryville in Gaston County on June 26, 2025, at 4:16 PM EDT. Cherryville is the place named in that report. Do not read it as hail on every Gastonia street. A neighbor’s bruises do not prove yours, and a quiet afternoon does not prove the roof was spared.

Our storm damage inspections are free. You get photos and a repair, replace, or leave-it answer. If an opening is letting rain in, emergency tarping can cover it until you choose the permanent work. A tarp is temporary. It is not the roof.

Permits on a Gastonia roof

The building permit goes through Gaston County Building & Development Services. North Carolina G.S. 143-138(b5) does not require a permit for single-family work at or under the amount in that statute when the work meets current code, unless a listed trigger applies. Under that amount, the county still requires a residential permit if the job includes structural, electrical, mechanical, or plumbing work. Inside Gastonia city limits, zoning is the city and the building permit is the county.

Gutters and RainDrop guards

Gutters are how water is supposed to leave the house, and they are separate from the shingles. We install, repair, and clean them on their own. RainDrop gutter guards are optional. They are not included with the roof, and they appear on the estimate only if you ask for them. See gutters.
