Standing seam panel types

Architectural and structural standing seam from the MRS standing seam category. Materials commonly include Galvalume, Aluminum, and Copper with Kynar 500 (PVDF) finish — as published on each product page. Official links open in a new tab.

1K

System 1000 — 1.5″ Snap Lock

Snap-lock architectural panel with concealed clips — efficient install and weathertight protection. Can transition roof to fascia or mansard. Typical min. pitch 3:12 over solid substrate.

MRS System 1000 →
1.5K

System 1500 — 1.5″ Mechanical Lock

Mechanically seamed panel (90° / 180° locks) for historical field-formed aesthetics, high wind loads, and low-slope performance. Typical min. pitch 1:12; field seamer required.

MRS System 1500 →
2K

System 2000 — 1.75″ Structural Snap-Lock

Structural 1.75″ integral snap-lock — weathertight joint without a mechanical seamer, with strong spanning capability. Typical min. pitch 3:12 over solid substrate.

MRS System 2000 →
2.5K

System 2500 — 2″ Mechanical Lock

2″ mechanically seamed structural panel for high wind and low slope (min. pitch as low as 1/2:12). Install over solid substrate or open framing; single or double lock with field seamer.

MRS System 2500 →
3K

System 3000 — Mechanical / low slope

Mechanically seamed system suited to low-slope applications. Formed in steel, aluminum, or copper; solid substrate or open framing. See MRS for profile and project options.

MRS System 3000 →
+
