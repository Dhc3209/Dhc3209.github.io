Brava slate

Brava Slate / Old World Slate

Brava slate composite delivers the depth and shadow lines of natural slate in a polymer profile that is lighter than quarried stone — easier on structure, with Class 4 impact and hail-warrantied options when specified.

Why homeowners choose Brava Slate

  • Natural slate appearance with ~1″ profile depth
  • Class 4 impact ratings available; Class A or C fire options
  • Colorcast mineral-infusion color that holds up outdoors
  • Recycled / sustainable content in the composite mix
  • Extended transferable 50-year limited warranty (manufacturer terms)

CPR installs Brava as a secondary materials option alongside our primary GAF Master Elite systems (Timberline HDZ, Grand Sequoia, Royal Sovereign). We’ll help you compare look, weight, and warranty at inspection — no pressure to pick a brand that doesn’t fit the home.
