Why CPR

Local ownership. Factory credentials.

Daniel H. Campbell III founded Campbells Precision Roofing in 2016. A graduate of Appalachian State University (Physics) and raised in Blowing Rock, NC, he built the company around honest inspections, clean installs, and people who show up.

Today CPR is a licensed, insured GAF Master Elite contractor (President’s Club, FORTIFIED Roof capable) — installing Timberline HDZ, Grand Sequoia, Royal Sovereign, and qualifying Golden Pledge® systems. We also hold CertainTeed SELECT ShingleMaster and VELUX credentials, and install Brava composite profiles when they fit the project — serving Denver and the Lake Norman region across six counties.
