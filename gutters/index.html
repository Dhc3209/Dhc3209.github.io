Profiles & sizes we install

We help pick the right profile and size for your roof and rainfall — modest, property-first install, no invented certifications.

HR

Seamless 6″ half-rounds

Classic half-round profile, fabricated seamless for a clean look and fewer leak points along the run.

5″

5″ gutters

A common residential size — solid capacity for many homes when matched with proper downspouts and pitch.

6″ gutters

Extra capacity for larger roof areas or heavier rainfall — a frequent upgrade where 5″ runs short.
