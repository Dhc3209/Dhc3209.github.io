GAF warranties

Enhanced GAF warranties through a Master Elite® contractor

Campbells Precision Roofing is a GAF Master Elite® contractor (ID 1114013). That status lets us offer GAF’s enhanced limited warranties on qualifying residential systems — not every roof automatically gets the top tier.

Important eligibility caveats: Coverage depends on product selection, the number of qualifying GAF accessories installed, property type (e.g., single-family detached vs. other buildings), proper installation, and — for enhanced warranties — registration by your GAF certified contractor. Terms, exclusions, and transfer rules are set by GAF. This page is a plain-language overview; see official GAF documents for complete coverage and restrictions. We confirm which tier your home can qualify for at estimate.

Official comparison and documents: GAF residential warranties · qualifying Lifetime Roofing System components: gaf.com/LRS.

Golden Pledge® Limited Warranty

Who can offer it: GAF Master Elite® contractors only.

Typical system path: Qualifying Timberline® or Designer shingles (note: Timberline® NS is excluded from Golden Pledge on Timberline paths) plus at least 5 qualifying GAF accessory products.*

Highlights (qualifying systems): Lifetime† materials coverage with a long non-prorated Smart Choice® period (up to 50 years on published residential charts). Workmanship / misapplication coverage is often 25 or 30 years non-prorated depending on shingle line and property type, with extended lifetime workmanship on eligible single-family detached homes — see GAF’s current comparison for your product.

Tear-off and disposal may be covered when necessary under this tier. We confirm eligibility at your free estimate.

Silver Pledge™ Limited Warranty

Who can offer it: GAF Certified Plus or Master Elite® contractors.

Typical system path: Qualifying shingles plus at least 4 qualifying GAF accessory products.*

Highlights (qualifying systems): Enhanced manufacturing coverage similar to System Plus, plus workmanship / misapplication coverage (published terms commonly 10 or 15 years depending on Timberline® line; Designer paths list non-prorated workmanship periods — verify on GAF’s chart for your shingles).

Tear-off and disposal may be covered when necessary. Available through CPR on qualifying installs.

GAF warranty comparison →

System Plus Limited Warranty

Who can offer it: GAF Certified, Certified Plus, or Master Elite® contractors.

Typical system path: Qualifying shingles plus at least 3 qualifying GAF accessory products.*

Highlights (qualifying systems): Stronger manufacturing-defect protection than the base / Roofing System path — including a longer non-prorated Smart Choice® period (up to 50 years on published residential charts) and labor toward covered manufacturing remedies during that period, plus tear-off and disposal when necessary. Does not include workmanship / misapplication coverage (that starts at Silver Pledge™ / Golden Pledge®).

GAF Lifetime† Roofing System

Install GAF Lifetime† shingles with at least 3 qualifying accessory products* and you may be covered under the GAF Roofing System Limited Warranty (lifetime† on qualifying accessories as well as shingles) — registration is not required for this base system path.

WindProven™ Limited Wind Warranty: Available when you install LayerLock®-labeled shingles with the required accessory set (GAF publishes at least four accessories including starter, ridge cap, roof deck protection, plus attic ventilation or leak barrier). WindProven™ has no maximum wind speed limitation for covered LayerLock® shingles under GAF’s published terms — subject to installation and eligibility rules.

President’s Club Limited Warranty (optional note)

GAF also publishes a President’s Club Limited Warranty for qualifying systems installed by a current 3-Star President’s Club award-winning Master Elite® contractor, with additional eligibility limits on shingle lines and accessories. CPR holds President’s Club recognition; whether a given project can use this path depends on GAF’s current award status, product mix, and registration. Ask at estimate — we will not promise this tier unless GAF’s rules for your job are met. Details: GAF residential warranties.

What usually decides your tier

  • Contractor certification — Master Elite® can offer Golden Pledge®; lower certifications cannot.
  • Accessory count — typically 3 (System Plus / Roofing System), 4 (Silver Pledge™), or 5 (Golden Pledge® / President’s Club paths).
  • Shingle choice — some lines are excluded from certain tiers (e.g., Timberline® NS on Golden Pledge Timberline charts); workmanship lengths vary by product.
  • Property type — “Lifetime†” as GAF defines it generally applies to single-family detached residences owned by individuals; other building/owner types often have a fixed term (commonly 40 years on GAF charts).
  • Registration — enhanced warranties (System Plus, Silver Pledge™, Golden Pledge®, President’s Club) must be registered; your GAF certified contractor handles that after install.

* Qualifying accessories typically include GAF ridge cap, starter strip, leak barrier, roof deck protection, and attic ventilation products. Full lists: gaf.com/LRS. Low-slope membranes are covered under separate GAF warranties.

† Lifetime means as long as the original owner(s) (or a properly transferred second owner during the Smart Choice® period) own the property where the products are installed — for eligible single-family detached homes owned by individuals. See GAF’s limited warranty text for the exact definition and any shorter term for other property types.
