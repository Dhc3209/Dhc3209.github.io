Brava Spanish

Brava Spanish / Barrel Tile

Brava Spanish composite recreates classic clay Spanish and barrel profiles in a lightweight synthetic that avoids the heavy structural support and breakage risk of real clay tile.

Performance highlights

  • Clay Spanish / barrel look without clay weight
  • Class A/C fire ratings and Class 4 impact options
  • Engineered for high-wind regions when fastened to Brava’s published schedules
  • Limited 50-year warranty (manufacturer terms)

This is an available materials path at CPR — our lead systems remain GAF Master Elite (Timberline HDZ, Grand Sequoia, Royal Sovereign, Golden Pledge® on qualifying jobs). Ask whether Brava Spanish fits your architecture and budget.
