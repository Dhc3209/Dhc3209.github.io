How Much Does a Roof Replacement Cost in Charlotte, NC?

Typical 2026 price ranges by material, and the factors that move a Charlotte-area roof replacement quote up or down.

Roof replacement cost in the Charlotte area depends mostly on roof size and material. As a rough guide, typical installed ranges for a 20-square (about 2,000 sq ft) asphalt roof run from about $7,000 for basic 3-tab to about $14,000 for architectural shingles. Metal costs more (see the table below). Rotten decking, steep-pitch labor, and permit and disposal fees are usually priced separately and can raise the final number.

Short version

  • Typical asphalt shingle replacement on a 20-square roof: about $7,000–$14,000, depending on 3-tab vs architectural.
  • Standing seam metal typically costs roughly twice as much as architectural shingles.
  • Hidden items like rotten decking, steep pitch, and permits can add to the base bid, so ask how they’re priced up front.
  • After hail or wind, Campbells Precision Roofing inspects and photo-documents the damage, and any replacement is a full tear-off down to the deck.

Why this matters

Charlotte and the Lake Norman area see hail and damaging wind most often in spring and summer, and storm damage is a common reason homeowners here replace a roof before it’s worn out. Demand also matters: after a big hailstorm, contractor schedules fill quickly.

Certification matters for warranties. Any roofer can install a GAF shingle that comes with GAF’s standard limited warranty, but GAF’s enhanced warranties depend on the installer’s certification level, and Golden Pledge® requires a Master Elite® contractor.

How much does a roof replacement cost in Charlotte, NC?

Roofers price jobs by the “square,” which is 100 square feet of roof surface. A typical single-family home in the Charlotte area has roughly 20 to 30 squares (2,000 to 3,000 square feet). Material choice moves the total more than any other single factor.

MaterialTypical cost per square (installed)Typical total, 20-square (≈2,000 sq ft) roof
3-tab asphalt shingle$350–$500$7,000–$10,000
Architectural (dimensional) shingle$450–$700$9,000–$14,000
Standing seam metal$900–$1,500$18,000–$30,000

Ranges are general planning figures, not a quote. Your price depends on the roof’s size, pitch, layers, decking, and the system you choose.

Verdict: architectural shingles sit in the middle of the market and are the usual middle-ground choice for a Charlotte roof replacement in 2026. Metal earns its higher price mainly when you plan to stay in the house long-term.

Asphalt (3-tab) shingle roof replacement: typically $7,000–$10,000

Basic 3-tab shingles are the lowest-cost option and quick to install on a simple roof. Best for: homeowners who need a functional roof on a tight budget. The tradeoff is a flatter look, shorter warranties, and lower wind performance than architectural shingles.

Architectural shingle roof replacement: typically $9,000–$14,000

Dimensional shingles like GAF Timberline HDZ® cost more per square but offer higher wind ratings and longer warranties, which matters in a storm-prone area. Best for: most Charlotte-area homeowners doing a standard replacement.

Metal roof replacement: typically $18,000–$30,000

Standing seam metal typically costs about twice as much as architectural shingles. In exchange, you get a long service life and panels that don’t crack or shed granules in hail. Best for: homeowners planning to stay long-term, or whose shingle roof has had repeat hail damage. Skip it if budget is the only driver, since the upfront gap is real. See our metal roofing options.

Why roof replacement costs vary in Charlotte

  • Roof pitch and access: steep or multi-story roofs need more safety setup and slow the crew down.
  • Decking condition: every replacement we do is a full tear-off down to the deck, never an overlay, so rotten plywood gets found and replaced. That adds material and labor that wasn’t in the base price.
  • Material choice: shingles and standing seam metal span a wide price range, as shown above.
  • Permit and disposal fees: local permit costs (Mecklenburg, Lincoln, and neighboring counties) plus dumpster and disposal.
  • Storm-season demand: busy periods after hail can tighten schedules.
  • Storm damage: if hail or wind is why you’re replacing, get the roof inspected and the damage photo-documented first. Questions about your policy go to your insurance company.

How long does a roof replacement take in Charlotte, NC?

It depends on the roof’s size, pitch, and complexity, the decking condition, and the weather. Ask each contractor for an expected start-to-finish timeline in writing. Standing seam metal generally takes longer than shingles, because panels are measured, fitted, and seamed individually.

Is metal roofing worth the extra cost in the Lake Norman area?

Using the typical ranges above, metal runs about $18,000–$30,000 versus about $7,000–$14,000 for shingles on a 20-square roof. It resists hail and wind well and lasts a long time. Verdict: worth it for long-term owners, and usually more than you need for a house you plan to sell soon.

One last thing

The line item that most often changes a roof replacement quote is rotten decking found during tear-off. Before you sign, ask how decking replacement will be priced (for example, a per-sheet rate in writing), not just the bottom-line total.
