Hail Damage Roof Insurance Claims in the Lake Norman Area

How Lake Norman homeowners document hail and wind roof damage for insurance — free inspection from a local GAF Master Elite crew.

Hail and wind don’t always leave a roof looking wrecked from the street. Around Lake Norman and Denver, NC, we see bruised shingles, lifted edges, and soft spots that only show up once you’re on the roof — and those are exactly the things insurance needs documented right.

What to do after a storm

Don’t wait on a vague “it looks fine.” Get the roof walked. We take photos, note granule loss and impact marks, and put together a clear report you can use with your adjuster. If you file and ask us to, we can be there when the adjuster inspects, to show what we documented. Start with our storm damage and insurance claims process, or contact us to schedule.

How the claim usually goes

  1. Free inspection and documentation
  2. You file (or we help you get the paperwork straight)
  3. Adjuster visit — we can be there if you ask us to
  4. Approved scope → we replace or repair with a proper GAF system when it’s a full roof

We’re a GAF Master Elite and President’s Club contractor based in Denver, NC. No pressure pitch, no deductible games — just an honest look at the roof and a clear plan.
