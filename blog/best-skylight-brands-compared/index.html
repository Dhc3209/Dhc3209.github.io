Best Skylight Brands Compared for 2026

Fixed, Fresh Air, and Sun Tunnel® skylights compared by a VELUX-certified installer, plus how to compare skylight quotes around Lake Norman.

A skylight isn’t just a window. It cuts through the roof covering, the deck, and the ceiling of the room below, so the install and flashing matter as much as the brand. We install VELUX, and in 2026 it’s still our default recommendation for homes around Denver, NC and Lake Norman. Here’s how to choose the right type first, then compare brands and quotes.

Short version

  • Daylight and a sky view, no ventilation: a VELUX fixed skylight.
  • Daylight plus fresh air: a VELUX “Fresh Air” venting skylight (manual, electric, or solar-powered).
  • Daylight in a hallway, closet, or small interior room: a VELUX Sun Tunnel® tubular skylight.
  • Choose the type before the brand, and make sure any quote names the exact unit and flashing kit.

Why this matters

A brand comparison that stops at glass and controls misses the decisions most likely to change your project: the roof opening, the flashing, the framing, and the finished ceiling. A skylight is only as watertight as its flashing and installation.

Campbells Precision Roofing is a VELUX-certified installer. We provide new skylight installs, replacements, and leak repair with the correct VELUX flashing kits. If a roof replacement is already planned, it’s the best time to add or replace a skylight, because the flashing ties in with the new roof.

What makes the best skylight?

  • Type of opening: fixed, venting, or tubular. They aren’t interchangeable.
  • Flashing: which flashing kit matches the unit and your roof covering, and who installs it.
  • Ventilation: whether the room needs air as well as light.
  • Room placement: ceiling, attic space, and framing between the roof and the room.
  • Installation scope: roof work and interior finishing, spelled out in writing.
  • Service path: who you call about a leak or operating problem, and which written warranty terms apply.

1. VELUX fixed skylights: best for daylight and a sky view

A fixed skylight is the simplest option when you want natural light and a view of the sky but don’t need the unit to open. VELUX offers deck-mounted (FS), curb-mounted (FCM), and self-flashed fixed styles, and the right one depends on your roof and the opening.

Pros: nothing to operate, and a clean look. Cons: no ventilation, and the route from roof to ceiling still has to work. Verdict: Buy when daylight is the only goal and the location works.

2. VELUX Fresh Air skylights: best for daylight plus ventilation

Fresh Air skylights open to let hot or humid air out. They come in manual, electric, and solar-powered versions. VELUX documents remote control and a rain sensor that closes the unit when rain is detected on its electric and solar models.

Pros: light and airflow from one opening, and powered models are easy to use on high ceilings. Cons: an operable unit adds a control decision, and a manual unit has to be within reach. Verdict: Buy for kitchens, baths, and upstairs rooms that trap heat.

3. VELUX Sun Tunnel® skylights: best for small interior spaces

A Sun Tunnel brings daylight from a small roof opening down a rigid or flexible tube to a ceiling diffuser. It’s not a window you look through, which makes it a good fit for hallways, closets, and interior bathrooms where a full skylight isn’t practical.

Pros: daylight where a conventional skylight won’t fit, from a small roof opening. Cons: no sky view and no ventilation. Verdict: Buy when daylight is the goal and you don’t need a view or airflow.

Comparing quotes that use other skylight brands

You may get quotes built on other manufacturers’ skylights. Compare them on the same points: the type of opening, the exact unit and size, the flashing kit for your roof covering, any framing work, interior finishing, and the written product and workmanship terms. Two quotes that propose different opening types aren’t direct alternatives, even if both promise more daylight.

How we ranked these options

Each type gets its own use case: daylight only, daylight plus air, and daylight in tight interior spaces. The ranking weighs the roof opening, flashing, ventilation, placement, install scope, and service path. It doesn’t claim any unit will outperform another on a roof nobody has looked at.

Which skylight should you choose?

Decide what the opening has to do first. Choose a VELUX fixed skylight for light and a view, a Fresh Air skylight if you also need ventilation, and a Sun Tunnel® for small interior spaces. Don’t sign off on any quote based on a brand name alone. Ask for the exact unit, its position, the flashing kit, and who handles the roof and interior work.

If your roof already leaks or has storm damage, get it inspected before treating a new skylight as the fix. FAQ

What is the best skylight brand in 2026?

We install VELUX and recommend it as the default for most homes. Whatever brand you consider, confirm the exact unit, placement, flashing kit, and install scope before you sign off.

Should I choose a fixed or venting skylight?

Choose fixed if daylight is the goal and you don’t need airflow. Choose a Fresh Air venting skylight for kitchens, baths, or rooms that trap heat, as long as you can operate it where it sits.

What is a Sun Tunnel skylight?

A VELUX Sun Tunnel® is a tubular skylight. It carries daylight from a small roof opening down a tube to a ceiling diffuser. It doesn’t give a view or ventilation.

Can I add a skylight during a roof replacement?

Yes, and it’s a good time to do it, since the flashing ties in with the new roof. Ask for the skylight, flashing, roof work, and interior finishing in one written scope.

Do you repair leaking skylights?

Yes. We diagnose and repair skylight leaks, and we’ll tell you whether the flashing, the unit, or the surrounding roof is the problem.

Does a GAF roof warranty cover my skylight?

Don’t assume it does. GAF’s warranties cover qualifying GAF roofing systems, and VELUX publishes its own skylight warranties. Ask which terms apply to each part of the job.

One last thing

The most useful question at the end of a skylight comparison isn’t which brand won. It’s: what exactly will be opened, flashed, and finished? If a quote can’t answer that for both the roof and the room, hold off, even if you like the brand. Planning a new roof too? See best roofing materials for Charlotte weather.
