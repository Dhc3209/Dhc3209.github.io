GAF Designer Collection shingles

Premium curb appeal from the GAF Designer Collection — luxury asphalt shingles that deliver wood-shake and slate looks without the weight or upkeep of natural materials. As a GAF Master Elite contractor, we install these lines as part of a complete GAF system for Denver & Lake Norman homes.

Grand Sequoia®

Lifetime† Roofing System

As GAF publishes: when you install GAF Lifetime Shingles and at least 3 qualifying GAF accessories, you get a Lifetime Limited Warranty on qualifying GAF products. †Lifetime refers to the length of warranty coverage and means as long as the original individual owner(s) of a single-family detached residence [or eligible second owner(s)] owns the property. For other owners/structures, Lifetime coverage is not applicable. See GAF’s Shingle & Accessory / Roofing System Limited Warranties for complete coverage and restrictions. Visit gaf.com/LRS for qualifying products.

StainGuard Plus™

Designer Collection lines are marketed with GAF Time-Release Algae-Fighting Technology that powers a 25-year StainGuard Plus™ Algae Protection Limited Warranty against blue-green algae discoloration — available only on products sold in packages bearing the StainGuard Plus™ logo. See the GAF Shingle & Accessory Limited Warranty for complete coverage and restrictions.

Eligibility caveats: Warranty and system coverage depend on the shingle line, qualifying accessories, ownership type, and package markings (e.g., StainGuard Plus™ logo). Golden Pledge® and other enhanced system warranties may apply on qualifying Master Elite installs — we’ll confirm eligibility at your inspection. Always verify current details on gaf.com.
