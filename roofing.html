How a CPR roof goes on

Clear steps from inspection to warranty

No mystery process — you’ll know what’s happening on the roof and why.

1. Free inspection

We document condition, explain repair vs replace in plain language, and map GAF system options that fit the house and budget.

2. Scope & materials

Shingle line (HDZ®, UHDZ®, Designer, or Royal Sovereign), underlayment, ventilation, and flashing — written clearly before work starts.

3. Install & cleanup

Master Elite standards on the deck, dry-in, and finish. Magnets and daily cleanup so the property isn’t left a mess.

4. Walkthrough & warranty

Final walkthrough, then paperwork for qualifying Golden Pledge® or other enhanced GAF warranties when the system earns them.
