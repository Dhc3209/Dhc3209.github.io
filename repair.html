Repair vs replace

An honest call — not an upsell script

We’ll show you what we find and recommend the smaller fix when it’s enough.

When repair makes sense

  • Isolated leaks or flashing failures
  • Limited storm damage on an otherwise sound roof
  • Age and remaining life still favor a targeted fix

When replacement is smarter

  • Widespread wear, curling, or granule loss
  • Multiple leak paths or deck concerns
  • Storm damage plus age that won’t outlast a patch

What you get either way

  • Photos and plain-language notes
  • Storm documentation when applicable
  • Cleanup and a final walkthrough

Next step
