VELUX Products

As a VELUX-certified installer, Campbells Precision Roofing installs and services VELUX skylights and Sun Tunnel® skylights the right way — watertight flashing, clean finishes, lasting daylight. GAF Master Elite stays our primary roofing credential elsewhere on the site; VELUX is our secondary skylight credential and product line.

What we deliver

  • New installs and replacements
  • Fixed, vented “Fresh Air,” and Sun Tunnel® options
  • Leak diagnosis and repair on existing skylights

Product families below use VELUX USA naming. We don’t invent SKUs or warranties here — manufacturer coverage is as published by VELUX; we confirm the right system at inspection.

Daylight without ventilation

Fixed skylights

When you want sky views and natural light without an operable sash — deck-mounted, curb-mounted, and self-flashed styles as published by VELUX USA.

FS

FS — Deck-mounted fixed

Deck-mounted fixed skylight for daylight and sky views. Maintenance-free look with a pre-finished white interior wood frame as described by VELUX.

VELUX FS fixed →
FCM

FCM — Curb-mounted fixed

Curb-mounted fixed system for daylight and sky views. No designated top, bottom, or sides — can be oriented to fit the opening as VELUX documents.

VELUX FCM fixed →
SF

Self-flashed fixed styles

Self-flashed fixed options (including families such as QPF / EF as listed by VELUX) sit on the roof deck with factory flashing approaches — useful when that install style fits the roof.

All VELUX skylight systems →

Daylight + ventilation

Vented “Fresh Air” skylights

Operable skylights that open for fresh air. Deck-mounted (VS / VSE / VSS) and curb-mounted (VCM / VCE / VCS) families as published by VELUX USA. Electric and solar models may include remote control and an acoustic rain sensor that closes the unit when rain is detected — as VELUX documents on those product pages.

VS

Manual — VS / VCM

Manual “Fresh Air” skylights open for ventilation and help with comfort and moisture balance. Deck-mounted VS and curb-mounted VCM.

VELUX Fresh Air systems →
VSE

Electric — VSE / VCE

Hardwired electric Fresh Air models (deck VSE / curb VCE). Controlled electrically; see VELUX product pages for remote and rain-sensor features on the specific model.

VELUX electric Fresh Air →
VSS

Solar — VSS / VCS

Solar-powered Fresh Air (deck VSS / curb VCS). A small solar panel powers open/close; VELUX documents an acoustic rain sensor that closes at the first sign of rain, plus remote control options.

VELUX VSS solar Fresh Air →

Tubular daylighting

Sun Tunnel® skylights

Cost-effective daylight for closets, hallways, bathrooms, and other interior rooms — reflective tubes bring roof light to the ceiling below. Rigid and flexible tube options as published by VELUX.

RT

Rigid Sun Tunnel®

Best when there is a relatively straight path from roof to ceiling. Brighter white light and strong performance on longer runs, per VELUX.

Rigid Sun Tunnel® →
FT

Flexible Sun Tunnel®

Easier when attic obstacles require bends. Maneuverable tubing for shorter, obstructed runs — as described on the VELUX Sun Tunnel® pages.

Sun Tunnel® overview →
