Factory credentials

Why CPR

Master Elite & President’s Club. Factory-certified. NC licensed.

These aren’t logos we bought — they’re earned credentials that decide who can stand behind the roof, the skylights, and the warranty.

  • GAF Master Elite® — GAF’s highest residential contractor certification. About 2% of roofers in North America. Invitation-only: licensed, insured, trained, and renewed each year. This is what lets us offer Golden Pledge® on qualifying GAF systems.
  • GAF President’s Club — Annual award on top of Master Elite. Fewer than half of Master Elites earn it. GAF recognizes performance, reliability, and service — not just the badge.
  • GAF Certified™ Commercial Contractor — Factory-certified for commercial GAF systems (TPO / PVC and more). Proper licensing and insurance required — we can offer GAF commercial system warranties on qualifying jobs.
  • FORTIFIED Certified — IBHS FORTIFIED® Roof–trained crew. We install to the FORTIFIED Roof standard so qualifying homes can earn the designation homeowners and insurers look for after hail and high wind.
  • VELUX Certified Technicians — Factory-trained for VELUX skylight install, replacement, and leak repair — so daylighting work matches the roof system around it.
  • Brava Craftsman installers — Certified to install Brava composite slate, Spanish tile, and cedar-shake profiles when that look and performance fit the home.
  • CertainTeed ShingleMaster PRO — CertainTeed’s PRO company ranking — advanced credentialing for CertainTeed shingle systems and the warranties that go with them.
  • NC Licensed Contractor #101889 — Licensed in North Carolina. Denver-based, insured local crew — not a storm-chaser franchise flipping leads.

FAQ

Questions homeowners ask

Where is Campbells Precision Roofing based?

Campbells Precision Roofing (Campbell's Precision Roofing LLC) is based in Denver, NC 28037. We serve Lincoln, Gaston, Mecklenburg, Catawba, Iredell, and Cleveland counties, with dedicated service pages for Denver, Cornelius, Huntersville, Mooresville, and Sherrills Ford, plus an Areas We Serve page for other communities.

What roofing services does Campbells Precision Roofing offer?

We provide roof replacement, roof repair, storm and hail damage assessment with insurance claims documentation, gutters, metal roofing, and skylights. We are FORTIFIED capable and VELUX Certified, with GAF as a primary manufacturer focus and CertainTeed and Brava as secondary options.

Is Campbells Precision Roofing a GAF Master Elite contractor?

Yes. Campbells Precision Roofing is a GAF Master Elite and GAF President's Club contractor (GAF ID 1114013). We also hold North Carolina Residential Contractor License #101889 and have maintained a BBB A+ rating since 2016.
