GAF’s #1-selling laminate

Timberline HDZ®

Modest summary from GAF’s Timberline HDZ® product page. Warranty and rating details depend on package markings and how the system is installed — we’ll confirm eligibility at your inspection.

HDZ

Peak performance homeowners trust

GAF markets Timberline HDZ® as its #1-selling shingle — engineered for dependable laminate performance with high-definition color and a wood-shake look. Two distinct color collections help complement a wide range of home styles.

LL

LayerLock® + StrikeZone®

Patented LayerLock® Technology powers StrikeZone® — which GAF describes as the industry’s widest nailing area — helping crews place nails accurately. GAF cites up to 99.9% nailing accuracy in its StrikeZone® testing (999 of 1,000 nails placed correctly in GAF’s test).

SG

StainGuard Plus™

GAF Time-Release Algae-Fighting Technology powers a 25-year StainGuard Plus™ Algae Protection Limited Warranty against blue-green algae discoloration — available only on products sold in packages bearing the StainGuard Plus™ logo. See GAF’s Shingle & Accessory Limited Warranty for complete coverage and restrictions.

C3

Class 3 impact (UL 2218)

Where GAF states it: Timberline HDZ® shingles pass the UL 2218 Class 3 Impact-Resistance Test and may qualify for insurance discounts where available. Only products sold in packages bearing the UL 2218 Class 3 PRI Validation Program statement are rated Class 3. Testing is under controlled lab conditions; hail damage is not covered under the limited warranty. Contact your insurer about any discount.

Premium Dual Shadow Line

Timberline UHDZ® with UltraMat™

Premium laminate from GAF for homeowners ready to go beyond HDZ — richer dimension, Class 4 impact where packages qualify, and extended algae coverage as GAF states.

UHD

Dual Shadow Line

GAF’s patented Dual Shadow Line precisely distributes color granules to create two parallel shadow lines — a thicker, ultra-dimensional wood-shake look GAF compares to dramatic “sunset shadows” that last all day. Proprietary granule technology and unique color blends help the roof look its best from any angle.

UM

UltraMat™ · Class 4 impact

UltraMat™ high-performance fiberglass mat technology enhances the shingle at its core and achieves a UL 2218 Class 4 impact-resistance rating — the highest class in that test. Only Timberline UHDZ® packages bearing the UltraMat™ logo and UL 2218 Class 4 Classification statement are rated Class 4. Lab testing; GAF makes no express or implied warranty about hail performance; hail damage is not covered under the limited warranty.

PRO

StainGuard Plus PRO™ — up to 30 years

Time-release algae-fighting technology helps unlock a 30-year StainGuard Plus PRO™ Limited Warranty against blue-green algae discoloration (GAF notes ~10% more algae-fighting technology than StainGuard Plus™-labeled shingles). Available only on products sold in packages bearing the StainGuard Plus PRO™ logo. See GAF’s Shingle & Accessory Limited Warranty for complete coverage and restrictions.

GP

Golden Pledge® · Master Elite

GAF Master Elite® contractors can offer up to 30 years of workmanship (misapplication) coverage when they install a qualifying GAF roofing system with Timberline UHDZ® and register a Golden Pledge® Limited Warranty. A qualifying system typically includes Timberline UHDZ® plus five qualifying GAF accessories (ridge cap, attic ventilation, starter strip, roof deck protection, and leak barrier). Coverage terms differ for non–single-family properties. See the Golden Pledge® Limited Warranty and gaf.com/LRS for complete coverage and restrictions. CPR is a GAF Master Elite contractor (ID 1114013) — we’ll confirm eligibility at your inspection.

Side by side

HDZ® vs UHDZ® — short comparison

Bullets adapted from GAF’s comparison article and product FAQs. Both lines share LayerLock® / StrikeZone® installation technology; UHDZ® adds premium aesthetics and higher-rated impact/algae features where packages qualify.

Timberline HDZ®

  • GAF’s #1-selling laminate — dependable high-definition look
  • LayerLock® + StrikeZone® nailing area
  • Up to Class 3 impact (UL 2218) where package-marked
  • Up to 25-year StainGuard Plus™ algae limited warranty (logo packages)
  • Typically the more affordable of the two
  • Broader color choice on the HDZ line (per GAF)

Timberline UHDZ®

  • Patented Dual Shadow Line — thicker, ultra-dimensional look
  • Same LayerLock® + StrikeZone® installation tech
  • UltraMat™ — up to Class 4 impact (UL 2218) where package-marked
  • Up to 30-year StainGuard Plus PRO™ algae limited warranty (logo packages)
  • Premium price point vs HDZ
  • Eligible for enhanced Golden Pledge® workmanship on qualifying Master Elite systems

Shared wind coverage (both lines): When installed with the required combination of four qualifying GAF accessories, GAF shingles with LayerLock® Technology can qualify for the WindProven™ Limited Wind Warranty (15-year) with no maximum wind speed limitation. Accessories typically include starter strips, roof deck protection, ridge cap shingles, and leak barrier or attic ventilation. See GAF Roofing System Limited Warranty for complete coverage and restrictions.

Eligibility caveats: Impact class, algae warranty term, WindProven™, Lifetime† system coverage, and Golden Pledge® all depend on package markings, qualifying accessories, ownership type, and registration. Always verify current details on gaf.com. We’ll walk through what applies to your roof at inspection.

Sources: Timberline HDZ® · Timberline UHDZ® with UltraMat™ · GAF comparison article.
