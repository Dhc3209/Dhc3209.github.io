Brava Cedar Shake

Brava Cedar Shake mimics hand-split cedar in a polymer composite — multi-width pieces, straight or staggered installs, roughly ⅝″–⅞″ thickness — so you get shake character without the maintenance of real wood.

Why Brava Cedar Shake

  • Hand-split cedar appearance in polymer composite
  • Maintenance-minded vs. real wood shake
  • Multi-width; straight or staggered courses
  • Limited 50-year warranty (manufacturer terms)
  • Title 24–oriented options where applicable

For many Lake Norman homes our primary recommendation is still a GAF Master Elite system (including Grand Sequoia designer looks). Brava Cedar Shake is available when you want a composite shake profile — we’ll spell out tradeoffs at your free inspection.
