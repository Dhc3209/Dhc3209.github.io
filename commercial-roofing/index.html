GAF commercial single-ply

EverGuard® TPO & PVC

Framed from GAF’s commercial roofing materials pages. Official links open in a new tab — no invented product SKUs beyond named EverGuard® lines.

TPO

EverGuard® TPO

GAF describes TPO as a highly engineered single-ply membrane for low-slope roofs — excellent performance at a cost-effective price. Heat-welded seams provide superior strength versus taped seams. GAF also highlights long-term heat and UV resistance, natural fungal resistance, and inherent flexibility without plasticizers.

  • Low-slope single-ply system
  • Heat-welded seams
  • Heat / UV resistance; reflective white surface options
  • Cost-effective performance per GAF

Where a full tear-off isn’t the right move, GAF notes that recovering with EverGuard® Fleece-back single-ply TPO can add years to a roof’s life in a fraction of the time and expense of a full reroof — we’ll confirm recover suitability on site.

Based on contractor certification, GAF states an EverGuard® TPO roofing system may come with a guarantee of up to 25 years. Eligibility depends on the system and contractor certification.

PVC

EverGuard® PVC

EverGuard® PVC (polyvinyl chloride) single-ply offers tested performance including durability, seam strength, long-term weathering, UV resistance, mold resistance, and increased protection against chemicals compared to many common roofing technologies — as published by GAF.

  • Heat-welded seams; high flexibility for difficult contours
  • Chemical / oil / grease resistance framing suited to restaurants and industrial settings (per GAF PVC materials messaging)
  • Long-term weathering and UV resistance
  • Reflective white surface options for potential cooling benefits

GAF warranties and guarantees do not provide coverage against chemical exposure or grease — refer to gaf.com for coverage and restrictions. We discuss real-world exposure (kitchens, industrial process areas) at inspection.

GAF lists PVC roofing membranes as suitable for single-ply systems with guarantees up to 30 years on qualified systems (additional requirements apply). Eligibility depends on the system and contractor certification.

PVC KEE & guarantees

PVC KEE (optional note)

On its commercial materials overview, GAF lists single-ply options including TPO, PVC, and PVC KEE. PVC KEE (ketone ethylene ester) is a PVC variant GAF describes for applications calling for increased protection against chemicals such as grease, animal fats, and jet fuel. Ask at inspection if a KEE membrane fits your building.

Guarantees — “up to” language

As GAF publishes: EverGuard® TPO systems may qualify for guarantees of up to 25 years; PVC systems for guarantees of up to 30 years on qualified systems. Additional requirements apply. Eligibility depends on the roofing system and contractor certification — we’ll confirm what may apply to your project; we don’t invent NDL or coverage details beyond GAF’s published framing.
