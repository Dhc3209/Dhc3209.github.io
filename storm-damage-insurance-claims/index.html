Inspect and photograph

We inspect the roof system (covering, flashing, vents, penetrations, and related components) and photograph what we find. You’ll get a clear explanation of what we saw — storm-related damage versus normal wear — in plain language.

What you get after the inspection

Clear photos you keep, a plain-language summary of what we found, and our recommendation: a targeted repair when that’s the honest answer, or a full replacement when the roof is past saving. No pressure, and no obligation.

Matching and discontinued shingles

Storm damage often affects only part of a roof. Matching older shingles can be difficult when colors have aged or products have been discontinued. We’ll tell you honestly whether a repair will blend in or whether a full replacement makes more sense for the home.

Full tear-off, to code (NC Residential Code R908)

North Carolina Residential Code section R908 addresses reroofing. Under North Carolina’s amendment, a roof replacement includes removing the existing roof coverings down to the deck (R908.3), and a new layer can’t be added over two existing layers (R908.3.1.1). We always tear off to the deck, on every roof. We’ll verify existing layers at inspection and explain how code applies to your specific home — not as legal advice, but as practical guidance from a licensed contractor (NC Residential license #101889). For the exact code text on tear-off, underlayment, wind-rated shingles and flashing, see our NC roofing code guide.

Storm repairs vs. code-required items

A replacement roof has to meet the code in effect today, which can mean more than putting back what was there: labeled underlayment, wind-rated shingles and nailing, replacement of rusted or damaged flashing, and whatever the shingle manufacturer’s instructions require. Our estimate lists the code-required items separately from optional upgrades, so you can see what the code requires and what’s your choice. See the NC code requirements for a roof replacement.

FAQ

Storm damage inspection questions

What does a free storm damage inspection include?

We check the shingles, flashing, vents, pipe boots, and gutters for hail and wind damage and any spots where water is getting in, photograph what we find, and explain storm damage versus normal wear in plain language. There’s no cost and no pressure.

Will I get photos of the damage?

Yes. You get clear photos of what we found and a plain-language repair-or-replace recommendation you can keep for your records.

What happens if my roof needs replacing?

It’s a full tear-off down to the deck, never new shingles over an old roof. We replace damaged decking, install new underlayment and flashing to code, and put on a complete GAF system with a GAF Master Elite® crew.

Are you an insurance company or a public adjuster?

No. Campbells Precision Roofing is a licensed roofing contractor (NC Residential #101889). We inspect, photograph, repair, and replace roofs. Questions about your policy go to your insurance company.

Which areas do you serve for storm damage roofing work?

We serve Lincoln, Gaston, Mecklenburg, Catawba, Iredell, and Cleveland counties in North Carolina, including Denver, Cornelius, Huntersville, Mooresville, and Sherrills Ford. Contact us at (704) 280-5996 to confirm service for your address.
